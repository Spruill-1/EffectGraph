# Contributing

EffectGraph is licensed under [MIT-0](LICENSE): anyone may use, copy, modify and
ship it, commercially or not, without attribution or any other condition. Every
contribution has to keep that true.

- **Contributions are accepted under MIT-0.** By opening a pull request you
  agree that your contribution is licensed under the same terms as the project,
  and that you have the right to license it so (it is your own work, or your
  employer has agreed).
- **No third-party code.** Do not copy in code, shaders, data tables or assets
  from elsewhere, even under a permissive license: MIT, BSD and Apache all
  require a notice that EffectGraph's users would then have to carry. Write it
  from the specification or the paper instead, and cite the source in a comment.
- **Dependencies** are the Windows SDK, the C++ standard library and
  C++/WinRT. Adding one is a design change; open an issue first.

Build, test and conventions: [docs/development/build.md](docs/development/build.md)
and [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md). Releases: [docs/development/releasing.md](docs/development/releasing.md).
